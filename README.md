<h1>Hey, I'm Navid 👋</h1>

I'm the kind of person who opens one tab to learn something and ends up with fifty. Curiosity usually wins.

My interests are all over the place, from **AI, technology, startups, and products** to understanding why some products become part of our everyday lives while others disappear without a trace.

I'm fascinated by how things work behind the scenes. How does Google understand a search? How do AI models find answers? What makes a product worth using? And why do some simple ideas end up changing the world?

I enjoy experimenting with tools, exploring open-source projects, testing ideas, and occasionally building something just to see if I can make it work. Some experiments succeed, some don't, and a few leave me with more questions than answers.

I have a soft spot for products that make life simpler. **UPI and WhatsApp** are two of my favourites. Nothing unnecessarily complicated, just technology that works.

Outside the digital world, I enjoy reading, discovering different perspectives, and questioning things most people take for granted.
